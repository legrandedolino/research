# Research
This is a repository of my files relevant to research projects.

## EFEC - Educating for Environmental Change
Files relevant to analysis of teacher-level data on outcomes of the five-year EfEC program, particularly the Summer Science Institute

## LONG - Design and Analysis of Reduced Longitudinal Experiments
Files relevant to Monte Carlo simulations and analysis of longitudinal experiments with less-than-ideal design conditions

Pre-print: [https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-7262280/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-7262280/v1)

## COND - Impacts of Conditional Model Specification to Large Scale Assessments
Files relevant to Monte Carlo simulations and analysis of impacts of Principal Component specification for IRT analysis of Large Scale Assessments, particularly in context of sub-group differences

Files are my versions; final code as used for publication submission may have shifted.
